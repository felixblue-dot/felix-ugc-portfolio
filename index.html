About

A bit about me

I'm Felix, 25, and I work from wherever I happen to be. I travel about half the year, and when I'm not on the road I'm based in Mannheim, Germany. I speak German and American English fluently, plus conversational Chinese.

I started creating UGC in 2026, after two years of hands-on social media work for other brands: building communities, running Meta ads and turning warm leads into paying customers. That work taught me what actually makes people stop scrolling and act, and I bring it into every video I make.

I script, shoot and edit everything myself. My focus is ad-ready creative built to perform on your own channels and as paid social. I work with brands on single videos or ongoing monthly content, depending on what you need.

Outside of content, I run an AI automation agency and have visited 70+ countries, about half of them solo. I'm especially keen on travel collaborations, so if you want someone who can turn a destination into a scroll-stopping story, let's talk.
