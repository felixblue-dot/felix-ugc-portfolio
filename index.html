How it works

From your email to an ad you can run.

  1. What you're promoting, who it's for and what the ad needs to do. Physical products get shipped to me, apps send a test login.

  2. The first half is invoiced on booking, the second half when you get the final files. Same for every package.

  3. Hooks, body and call to action. You don't need a script or a creative brief. You get an update when your package arrives.

  4. Every hook is a different opening on the same body, so you can test which one stops the scroll. You get an update when filming starts.

  5. Share it with your team and send notes. One revision round is included, two per video in Scale.

  6. No watermark, every hook as its own file, with and without captions. Usage starts the day you approve, the second 50% is invoiced on delivery.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Do you post the video on your own channel?

No, by default the videos are for your channels and your ads only. A feature on @felix.unstuck is possible for the right brand, case by case.

What do I need to send you?

The product (or a test login for apps) and a few lines on who it's for and what the ad should do. I write the script, so you don't need a creative brief.

How long can I use the videos?

3 months in Test and Growth, 6 months in Scale, on your own channels and in paid ads. The clock starts the day you approve the video. Longer usage is available on request.

Can you do whitelisting / Spark Ads?

Yes, quoted separately from the packages.

Which languages?

English and German, scripted and spoken natively.

How does payment work?

50% on booking, 50% on delivery, invoiced in EUR or your local currency.
